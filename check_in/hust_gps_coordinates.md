# HUST Campus Map – Approximate GPS Coordinates

Source: HUSTMap screenshot (Đại học Bách khoa Hà Nội), north at top.

> ⚠️ **These coordinates are ESTIMATES, not surveyed values.** I could not read GPS data from the HUSTMap screenshot or query Google Maps from here. I georeferenced the image by assuming ~1.45 m per pixel and anchoring the Tạ Quang Bửu Library at 21.0049, 105.8465. Expect errors of roughly 20–60 m. Use the Google Maps links to verify, and right-click the correct spot in Google Maps → click the coordinates to copy exact values.

Format: `latitude, longitude` (WGS84, decimal degrees).
